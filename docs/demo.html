Bluey for Windows demo: Bluey follows the mouse

Recorded on 2026-11-05. A Windows 11 laptop runs Bluey for Windows. An iPhone runs Bluey, installed with Sideloadly.

What the video shows

  • The iPhone stands in front of the laptop, in landscape. It shows Bluey.
  • The mouse cursor moves across the laptop screen. Bluey's eyes on the phone look toward the cursor.
  • Bluey's eyes are closed at the start (asleep), and open after that.

What it does not show

  • The video has no sound, so it does not show the chirp.
  • The phone text "Voice could not connect" is visible in the video. Voice was not working in this recording.
  • A blur hides the clock at the lower right of the laptop screen. The video is 10 seconds long, cropped from a longer recording.
  • The recording was made with a hand-held camera, so the picture moves a little.
